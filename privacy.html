Grapes Leaf Doctor – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Grapes Leaf Doctor is a free research application developed by Mrs. Madhuri Deepak Dharrao, PhD research scholar at Symbiosis International (Deemed University), Pune, India. It helps grape growers identify grape leaf diseases and gives weather-based disease alerts. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Photographs

Leaf photographs you take or choose are analysed only on your phone. They are never uploaded to any server and are not seen by the developer or anyone else.

Location

If you allow it, the app reads your phone's approximate location, or you can pick a village from a list instead. The location coordinates are sent to the weather services Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) and, if that fails, NASA POWER (power.larc.nasa.gov) only to download daily temperature and humidity for that place. No name, phone number or other identifier is sent with the coordinates.

Records on your phone

The results of your leaf checks (date, diagnosis, confidence, affected area and a small thumbnail), your chosen location and language are stored only on your phone, in the app's local storage. You can delete them at any time with "Clear records", or by clearing the app's data. You can export them as a CSV file, which is saved on your phone.

What we do not collect

The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

Advice disclaimer

The diagnosis and advice support, and do not replace, the advice of an agronomist or agricultural extension officer. Always follow the product label and the pre-harvest interval.

Children

The app is intended for grape growers and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
